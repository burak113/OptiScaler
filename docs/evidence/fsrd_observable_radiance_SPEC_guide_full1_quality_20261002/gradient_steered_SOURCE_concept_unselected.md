## One fixed observable law

Define M(p)=original full1 effective caller QcS(p)+QcD(p), RGB componentwise, using the same typed-load interpretation as caller composition. Define U(p)=observed original stored source C(p)/M(p). U is merely a visible normalization proxy: it need not equal one physical light, true irradiance, F0 or either lobe. Original full1 native signal/factor/guide/Skip resources stay unchanged.

Use one current-frame 5x5 separable binomial footprint b=(1,4,6,4,1), inherited from the existing spatial kernel, for all following moments. Central differences of U give RGB vectors gx,gy. Let K=weighted mean(gx gx^T+gy gy^T), a3x3 RGB second-moment matrix. Let J be the2x2 spatial second-moment tensor with entries weighted mean(dot(gx,gx)), weighted mean(dot(gx,gy)), weighted mean(dot(gy,gy)). No luma collapse or selected frequency is used.

Let lambdaMax,lambdaMin be J's eigenvalues. Spatial coherence a=(lambdaMax-lambdaMin)/(lambdaMax+lambdaMin), or0 for exactly zero trace. Let RGB agreement k=sum over c<d abs(Kcd) / sum over c<d sqrt(Kcc*Kdd), or0 for exactly zero denominator. Exact PSD arithmetic bounds both ratios in[0,1]; finite-precision implementations must preserve that bound or reject the sample, not fit a regularizer. Let w=a*k. This is an observable continuous weighting law, with no threshold, sweep or physical rank certificate. Independent RGB noise has zero population cross-channel terms under its stated independence; finite sample noise can still produce nonzero k and direction. Correlated stochastic illumination remains a counterexample. Real single-channel or multidirectional color detail can also have weak agreement: no general low-rank/shared-light assumption or guarantee follows.

Take t as J's minor-eigenvalue unit eigenvector (the local isophote tangent). Fline is the five-tap b-weighted mean of U at p+i*t, i=-2..2, using bilinear sampling; its sign does not matter. Fiso is the existing 5x5 separable b-weighted mean of U. When J's direction is exactly degenerate use w=0, so no arbitrary tangent contributes. Candidate visible total is M(p)*(w*Fline+(1-w)*Fiso).
